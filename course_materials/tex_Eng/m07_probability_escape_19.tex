\begin{enumerate}
	\item The statement "Data x follows a normal distribution" is written as $x \sim N(\mu,\sigma)$.
	\item Standardize the data. $z_i = \frac{x_i - \mu}{\sigma}$
	\item Since $z_i \sim N(0,1)$, use the characteristics of the standard normal distribution for inference.
\end{enumerate}
