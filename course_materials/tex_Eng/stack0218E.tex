\section{Summary}

This section is titled "summary" in English.
Let me summarize the story.

The normal distribution has two main meanings.
One advantage is that it can be evaluated based on the distribution of errors. Particularly, if it is understood how much random error is spread, it is possible to estimate this and manage the errors to some extent. The classical test theory model of data=true value+error assumes that errors are normally distributed. From the fact that the expected value is zero, it is believed that if there is enough data, it is possible to arrive at the true value.

Another way to understand the normal distribution is as a distribution that represents the overall tendency of a population. By focusing specifically on where the mean (expected value) is located, it becomes possible to grasp the trend. For example, by accumulating data in society, you can consider trends such as average height or average lifespan. This is a model that is relevant to all areas of psychology, and is based on the idea that individual characteristics are a combination of expected value and individual differences. By exploring and comparing the average trend, we use this overall characteristic to further discussions.

From the second meaning of the normal distribution (tendency of set data), we can use the characteristics of the normal distribution to estimate the relative position and proportion (e.g. around what percentile) of the data.
At that time, you can also calculate using the formula for the normal distribution with the mean and variance, but it can become complicated, so it is typical to standardize the data and interpret it by comparing it to the standard normal distribution.
Assuming a distribution, inferring the whole from a part of the data is one of the joys of psychological statistics.
