\begin{itemize}
    \item If other conditions are constant, as the significant level becomes smaller, it becomes less likely to become significant.
    \item If other conditions are constant, as the significant level becomes smaller, the probability of committing a type II error increases.
    \item If other conditions are constant, as the sample size increases, it becomes easier to become significant.
    \item If other conditions are constant, as the sample size increases, the absolute value of the theoretical statistical value when rejecting the null hypothesis becomes larger.
\end{itemize}
