\begin{itemize}
    \item Accuracy; 5 points is 5 points no matter who looks at it. It is suitable for objective and accurate descriptions, and can convey relatively certain facts. When compared to conveying information using everyday language such as "amazing" or "very amazing," the difference becomes clear.
    \item Comparability; 5 points is greater than 3 points. Predictions can be made based on probabilistic judgments. When considering comparing the degree of adverbs such as "dangerous" or "extreme" in everyday language, this also becomes clear.
    \item Summarizability; The overall data can be represented by representative values, etc. The characteristics and trends of the data can be understood using concise expressions. While expressions like "in short..." are used in everyday language, it is also possible to describe how the summary is being made.
    \item The model can be validated; Hypotheses can be tested by providing data to the model, and objective and rational conclusions can be drawn.
\end{itemize}
