Translation:

- ANOVA: Analysis of Variance
- Df: Degrees of Freedom
- SS: Sum of Squares
- MS: Mean Square
- F value: F-test statistic
- p: Probability value (also known as p-value)
- Condition: Independent variable or factor in the study
- Residuals: Variability in the dependent variable that is not accounted for by the independent variable(s)
