
Afterword for the First Edition

The various societal changes caused by the coronavirus infection that began in February of 2020 have also drastically changed the style of classes starting in the fiscal year 2021.
The university had to consider methods for conducting classes online and support its faculty in adapting to the change. As teachers, we were also forced to consider whether we could uphold the quality of our instruction without lowering our standards.

This text was born amidst such confusion.
Until then, the classes were conducted in a style where we referred to necessary reference materials as needed and delivered lectures using our own slides. However, it was thought that this approach had its limitations.
One issue is that it is difficult to adopt a style of referencing several books in different places when students cannot enter the campus.
There are also difficulties in using the style of adjusting the progress of lessons by looking at students' faces and supplementing them through lesson feedback. When online support began, there was a policy of avoiding large communication data sizes such as real-time video communication, taking into consideration the communication environment of students.

In fact, even before the issue of COVID-19, efforts were being made to create detailed lesson plans. This was because \citet{Ashida2019} made us realize the importance of a detailed lesson design, and we wanted to revise our overall lesson plan. When we began preparing for this, the COVID-19 issue emerged. Although the lesson plan was established before the start of the new school year, materials such as this text were not ready, and the plan to implement 15 lessons in each semester was not feasible. Therefore, it was not possible to immediately make use of it.

Upon reviewing the previous slide materials, it seemed that the content between bullet points was uneven and incomplete. Despite my best efforts in creating the classroom materials, upon closer inspection, many areas seemed confusing or relied on implicit assumptions. Thus, while it may have been too late for the first semester, the creation of new classroom materials began in the second semester, with the goal of transcribing all classroom content into written text.
This text is created by modifying and adjusting the transcription material prepared throughout the 2020 fall to 2021 spring semester. The line numbers are also included in this text as a feature to clearly indicate the reference point in the class and is one of the ideas presented in \citet{Ashida2019}.

This text is version-controlled. The current version is 2.0.1. The version number is composed of three elements: major, minor, and patch.
The current version is 2. If there are any changes to the outline of the syllabus, then the version number will increase by one. If there are any changes at the chapter level, then the minor number will increase.
Minor changes such as typos and errors will result in a change in the patch number. After all, this is my own text, so there are many typos, errors caused by conversion mistakes, mistakes in numbers and references. So, I have been repeatedly notified by my students and updated accordingly to get here.

The latest version of the syllabus and text are available on the website \footnote{\url{https://kosugitti.github.io/psychometrtics_syllabus/}}.
We use GitHub for management and create most of the figures using R. For typesetting, we of course use Lua\LaTeX.
As versions are frequently updated and there are advantages unique to PDF such as hyperlinking, I hope that electronic files can be utilized directly on electronic devices if possible.
However, it seems that many students are using printed copies, so in order to make it available as a book at cost, we have also prepared a paperback version using Kindle Direct Publishing.
This is a text prepared for students in the psychology program at the Faculty of Human Sciences at Senshu University, but it can be read by anyone. We have taken great care in the content to avoid misunderstandings and errors, but if you find anything strange, please contact the author.

The manuscript itself is provided under the Creative Commons BY-SA (CC BY-SA) license Version 4.0. We hope you will make use of it in accordance with the license, such as making modifications and redistributions.



A new fiscal year is about to begin. It will be the third season of classes, taking into consideration the measures against coronavirus.
Although there may be some areas that are insufficient or incomplete, I hope that this initiative will be of some help to all the students if only a little.
