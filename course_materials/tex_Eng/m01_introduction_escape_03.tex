\begin{itemize}
    \item Empiricism: astronomical records by Tycho Brahe and Kepler, various experiments by Galileo
    \item Quantitative approach (\keytermE{mathematical phenomenology}): descriptions by Galileo and Newton
    \item Mechanistic thinking: ideas represented by Descartes and Maxwell's Demon
    \item Element reductionism: ideas such as Descartes' "second principle" and "division and integration"
\end{itemize}
