\begin{enumerate}
	\item 全体平均(Grand Mean, GM) - The overall average
	\item 要因Aの効果の大きさ($|b_1|$) - Magnitude of the effect of Factor A ($|b_1|$)
	\item 要因Bの効果の大きさ($|b_2|$) - Magnitude of the effect of Factor B ($|b_2|$)
	\item 交互作用ABの効果の大きさ($|b_3|$) - Magnitude of the effect of interaction AB ($|b_3|$)
\end{enumerate}
